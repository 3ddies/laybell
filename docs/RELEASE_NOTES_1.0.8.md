# Laybell 1.0.8 — Release notes

1.0.8 supersedes the never-submitted 1.0.7, so for the stores it carries everything
since the last shipped build (1.0.6): beats direct-IAP, the 1.0.7 UI batch, the 1.0.8
batch, and the 2026-10-07 session. Draft "What's New" below.

---

## What's New (store copy)

**Buy beats in one tap.** Grab a beat the moment you hear it — the Shop now checks out
right in the app with Apple Pay / Google Pay. A new Shop card in your feed helps you
discover beats, songs and more to buy.

**Make your stories yours.** Add music and choose exactly where the song starts and how
long it plays. Draw and set a background on any story, rotate and move photos freely,
and line things up with the new center guides.

**See who unfollowed you.** Premium now surfaces who unfollowed you and who doesn't
follow you back, right from your followers and following lists.

**Daily badges.** Track your daily goals and streaks from your profile.

**Films.** Premium+ members can post landscape videos up to an hour long.

**Plus** the Hot Albums shelf, see-who-engaged on your posts, album tools, a smoother
camera and story experience, faster music, and lots of polish and fixes.
