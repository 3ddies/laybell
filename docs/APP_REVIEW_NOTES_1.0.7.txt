NOTIFICATIONS (Guideline 4.5.4)
Two reminders (a badge about to lapse; twelve days without opening the app) are OFF by default and need an explicit opt-in: an UNTICKED checkbox at onboarding profile setup, or Settings > Notifications > "Reminders from Laybell", also the opt-out. Activity notifications (likes, comments, follows, going live, a profile view by a friend) are not promotional. No push carries financial detail.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (Guideline 1.2)
- Automated filtering of objectionable terms on captions and comments.
- Reporting: the "..." menu on any post, profile, comment or message > Report.
- Blocking: the "..." menu > Block. Their posts leave the feed and Explore and they cannot make contact. Unblock from Settings > Blocked.
- Published contact information in the app and at laybell.app.

MINORS
Users under 18 are identified at signup. For them the app disables location capture and targeted advertising, defaults posts to friends-only, limits incoming messages to accounts they follow, blocks broadcasting, hides mature content, and excludes them from reminders and marketing email. Messaging and mature-content rules are enforced server-side.

PURCHASES (Guideline 3.1.1)
All digital purchases use In-App Purchase. Consumable credit packs at $4.99, $9.99, $19.99, $49.99 and $99.99 - Settings > Credits. NEW in 1.0.7: a beat in the Shop with a set price can also be bought directly with In-App Purchase (consumable products laybell_item_<cents>, $4.99-$999.99), as an alternative to spending credits. Credits are spent in-app (tips, Shop purchases, promoting a post) and cannot be converted back into money or transferred. Creator earnings are a separate balance, paid out through Stripe, never redeemable as credits.

SUBSCRIPTION
Two auto-renewing tiers in one group - Laybell Premium at $9.99/month and Laybell Premium+ at $19.99/month - Settings > Promotion Tools > the Premium card. Premium+ adds Films (landscape video up to one hour), removal of all advertising, and unlimited offline downloads.

FILMS
Landscape videos over nine minutes, for Premium+, on their own shelf in Laybell TV: Explore tab > TV icon, top right.

LIVESTREAMING
The LIVE button sits beside the home logo. Our provider records a broadcast only to serve it live; the recording is deleted when the broadcast ends - no replay is kept or offered.

MUSIC LICENSING
All audio is uploaded by users, who warrant they own or control the rights; Laybell hosts no commercial catalogue. Laybell LLC holds BMI and ASCAP public performance licences, has a DMCA agent registered with the U.S. Copyright Office, and enforces a published repeat-infringer policy.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Supabase (auth, database, storage); RevenueCat (entitlements); Cloudflare Stream (video, live); LiveKit (studio audio); Stripe Connect (payouts only); Resend (email); Google Cloud Translation; Tenor (GIFs); Expo/EAS (build, push). No AI services.

AVAILABILITY AND TRACKING
United States only. No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, so no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Ad personalisation is opt-in and off by default.
