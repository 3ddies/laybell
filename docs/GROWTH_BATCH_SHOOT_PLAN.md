# Laybell — Batch-Shoot Plan

**Goal:** film ~3 weeks of daily content in **3 sittings (~2.5 hrs total)** instead of
filming one video a day. Scripts live in [`GROWTH_VIDEO_SCRIPTS.md`](GROWTH_VIDEO_SCRIPTS.md);
posting dates live in [`content-calendar.csv`](content-calendar.csv). This file is the
**shooting order** — ignore the calendar day numbers while filming, just work down each session.

> **The one rule that decides what you can shoot today:** 8 of the 30 are **⚠️ REAL-DATA**
> (real producers, real payouts, real testimonials). You **cannot** film those now without
> inventing numbers — and fake earnings claims get you banned and burn trust. They're pulled
> into **Bucket R** at the bottom. Shoot them the day the real win lands. Everything in
> Sessions 1–3 you can film **today**.

## SESSION 1 — Face to camera (~40 min)

**Setup once, film 7 times.**
- **Wardrobe:** one outfit you like on camera. Keep it on the whole session so clips look like one era, not 7 different days.
- **Framing:** phone vertical on a tripod, lens at **eye level**, you centered or slightly off-center. Leave headroom for the on-screen hook text.
- **Light:** face a window or a ring light. Avoid a bright window *behind* you.
- **Audio:** this matters most. Use earbuds-with-a-mic or a $20 lav clipped under your shirt. Phone-across-the-room audio reads as amateur.
- **Teleprompter:** load the scripts into a free prompter app (BIGVU, Teleprompter by Vocal Image) OR tape bullet notes right beside the lens. Don't memorize — glance and talk.
- **Reset between takes:** say the hook, pause 1s, deliver. Re-roll the whole thing if you fumble; don't try to edit mid-take.

**Shoot in this order** (easy opinion takes first to warm up, your personal founder story last):

1. **Day 4** — "Stop giving your beats away free"
2. **Day 18** — "15-second clips aren't a career"
3. **Day 25** — "Spotify $0.003 vs fans $20"
4. **Day 20** — "Get your music heard in 2026"
5. **Day 11** — "BeatStars charges you to upload AND takes a cut" *(talk-to-camera part only — the screen inserts get grabbed in Session 2)*
6. **Day 13** — "Price your beats so people buy" *(talking part only — screen inserts in Session 2)*
7. **Day 30** — "I built this by myself" *(your founder story — most personal, save it for when you're loose)*

> **Tip:** record a plain 5-second "follow @laybelltv" outro once, in the same outfit — you can tack it onto the end of every video across all three sessions.

## SESSION 2 — Screen recordings (~60 min)

**No face, any outfit.** Just your phone screen + a voiceover. Use the iOS built-in screen
recorder (Control Center). You can narrate live or add the voiceover after.

**Before you start — stage the app once so every demo looks clean:**
- Sign in on the account whose screen looks best stocked.
- Have **@3ddie's shop** loaded with the on-tier beats (the "Cypher type beat" at Lease $9.99 / Buy $59.99 you used for Apple review is perfect B-roll).
- Clear notification clutter; turn on Do Not Disturb so nothing pops mid-record.
- Have a story-ready clip and a song queued for the story-music demo.

**Shoot grouped by app area** (minimizes navigating back and forth):

*Shop + buying flow (you're already in the shop):*
1. **Day 24** — "Your profile IS your store"
2. **Day 3** — "How fast you own a beat" *(tap beat → buy → files deliver)*
3. **Day 6** — "Sell your first beat (no BeatStars cut)"
4. **Day 27** — "Set up your beat shop in 5 minutes"
5. **Day 11 insert** — screen B-roll of your shop for the BeatStars take
6. **Day 13 insert** — screen B-roll of lease/exclusive pricing

*Feature demos:*
7. **Day 10** — "Score your story, pick the exact start" *(story music trim editor)*
8. **Day 17** — "Go live, get tipped in real time"
9. **Day 7** — "App built for music, not clips" *(quick montage: feed → Laybell TV → shop)*

*Community / recap (screen cuts, light voiceover):*
10. **Day 26** — "Genre community"
11. **Day 14** — "This week on Laybell" *(recap — refresh the real numbers the week you post it)*

> **Note:** Day 28 "Founding Producers shoutout" and Day 14 recap both name **real** handles/
> numbers on screen. Film the screen-capture skeleton now; drop in the real tags/stats the day
> you post. If you have zero real sellers to tag yet, hold Day 28 for Bucket R.

## SESSION 3 — Beat station (~45 min)

**At your pads/DAW, while you're actually making beats you'll list.** Film your hands + the
screen (overhead phone mount or a second angle). Use a trending beat-making sound; little/no talking.

**Do Day 16 first — it produces 3 beats you can reuse as the "listed" shots in the others.**

1. **Day 16** — "3 beats, 3 genres, 10 minutes" *(trap → lo-fi → drill)*
2. **Day 2** — "Trap type beat in 60 seconds, for sale before this ends"
3. **Day 9** — "Flip this sample: lease AND exclusive"
4. **Day 23** — "[Artist] type beat, one take" *(pick a popular artist your buyers search — name the file "[artist] type beat")*

> **Actually list the beats you make.** Every one of these ends on "listed, live right now." If
> the shop is real when the viewer checks, the video converts. If it's empty, it doesn't.

## BUCKET R — Real-data videos (shoot the day the win is real) ⏳

**Do not fake these.** Each needs a genuine producer, payout, or testimonial. Keep them on a
list and film within 24 hrs of the win while it's fresh and the person's excited.

| Day | Video | What unlocks it |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | "Made $240 in his sleep" | A real passive sale. *Until then:* run it as a **labeled demo** — "here's what happens when a beat sells" — not a claim. |
| **Day 15** | "First payout hit my bank" | **You can shoot this yourself** the first time *you* take a real Stripe payout. Blur the sensitive bits. Likely your fastest real-data unlock. |
| **Day 5** | "Meet [producer], 19, bedroom studio" | A real producer who'll let you feature them + a quote. |
| **Day 8** | "$1,000 her first month" | A real seller who actually hit it + her quote. |
| **Day 12** | "She posts her songs here" | A real singer/artist on the platform + quote. |
| **Day 19** | "Producer #2" | Second real producer feature. |
| **Day 22** | "200 followers, $600" | A real small-audience seller with real sales. |
| **Day 29** | "5 producers, 5 payouts, 1 app" | 5 real wallets/sales to cut together. |
| **Day 21** | Trend format | Not real-data — just **ad hoc**: ride whatever sound is trending that week, can't pre-batch. |

**How to source these fast:** the Day 28 "Founding Producers" offer (badge + Premium+ +
featured placement for early sellers) is your recruiting tool. Every producer who takes it is a
future Spotlight/Proof video. DM the outreach list in
[`outreach-tracker.csv`](outreach-tracker.csv).

## Recommended order of operations

1. **Session 2 first (screen recordings).** No face, no wardrobe pressure, and it forces you
   to stage the app cleanly — which you want done anyway. Lowest friction to start.
2. **Session 3 (beat station)** next time you're making beats regardless.
3. **Session 1 (face to camera)** when you've got 40 min, good light, and you feel on.
4. **Bucket R** rolls in continuously as real sales/producers show up. Day 15 (your own first
   payout) is probably the first real-data one you'll be able to shoot.

After each session, drop the clips into CapCut, add the on-screen hook text + captions, tack on
the follow-outro, and schedule them against [`content-calendar.csv`](content-calendar.csv).
