RESUBMISSION — WHAT CHANGED SINCE BUILD 17 (Submission ID 4fc33eca-c022-4f46-be7e-00046840232c)
1) Guideline 5.1.1(iv) — Privacy, contacts/location permission. FIXED in this build (1.0.8, build 18). The onboarding "Permissions" step no longer has a "Skip for now" button, and its "Continue" button now always proceeds to the system permission prompt for location and contacts. The user always reaches the iOS prompt and keeps full control there (they may choose "Don't Allow"). Location is not requested for self-identified minors, which is a separate legal requirement (see MINORS below).
2) Guideline 2.1 — "Does the app upload users' contacts to its server?" No. Contacts access is optional (the user taps "Enable" and grants the iOS permission) and is used only to find which of their contacts already have a Laybell account. The address book is read on-device; each phone number and email is normalized and one-way SHA-256 hashed locally; only those irreversible hashes are sent to our server to match against existing accounts. Raw contact data — names, phone numbers, emails — never leaves the device and cannot be recovered from the hashes.
3) Guideline 2.1(b) — Locating the In-App Purchases. Steps below. The Account Holder has accepted the Paid Apps Agreement (Laybell LLC).

PURCHASES (Guideline 3.1.1)
All digital purchases use In-App Purchase. Consumable credit packs at $4.99, $9.99, $19.99, $49.99 and $99.99 - Settings > Credits. A beat in the Shop with a set price can also be bought directly with In-App Purchase (consumable products laybell_item_<cents>), as an alternative to spending credits. Credits are spent in-app (tips, Shop purchases, promoting a post) and cannot be converted back into money or transferred. Creator earnings are a separate balance, paid out through Stripe, never redeemable as credits.

SUBSCRIPTION
Two auto-renewing tiers in one group - Laybell Premium $9.99/month and Laybell Premium+ $19.99/month - Settings > Promotion Tools > the Premium card. Premium+ adds Films (landscape video up to one hour), removal of all advertising, and unlimited offline downloads.

NOTIFICATIONS (Guideline 4.5.4)
Two reminders (a badge about to lapse; twelve days without opening the app) are OFF by default and need an explicit opt-in: an UNTICKED checkbox at onboarding, or Settings > Notifications > "Reminders from Laybell". Activity notifications (likes, comments, follows, going live) are not promotional. No push carries financial detail.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (Guideline 1.2)
Automated filtering of objectionable terms on captions and comments. Report: the "..." menu on any post, profile, comment or message > Report. Block: the "..." menu > Block. Published contact information in the app and at laybell.app.

MINORS
Users under 18 are identified at signup. For them the app disables location capture and targeted advertising, defaults posts to friends-only, limits incoming messages to accounts they follow, blocks broadcasting, hides mature content, and excludes them from reminders and marketing email. Enforced server-side.

MUSIC LICENSING
All audio is uploaded by users, who warrant they own or control the rights. Laybell LLC holds BMI and ASCAP public performance licences, has a registered DMCA agent, and enforces a repeat-infringer policy.
