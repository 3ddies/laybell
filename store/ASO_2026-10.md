## iOS — APP STORE CONNECT

### App Name (max 30)
**Recommended (keep):** `Laybell: Music & Creators`  *(25)*
Holds "music" + "creators" in the highest-weighted field, so the keyword slot doesn't
spend characters on them. Low risk, no rebrand.

**Optional, higher-intent:** `Laybell: Make Music & Beats`  *(27)*
Trades "creators" for the verb **make** + **beats** (make music, make beats — high intent),
and frees "beats" from the keyword field. Bigger ranking lever, but it's a visible brand
change. My call: keep "Music & Creators" for now; revisit if producer installs plateau.

### Subtitle (max 30)  ← the main change
**`Sell beats. Get paid. Go live.`**  *(exactly 30)*
Was: "Make music. Find your people." The new line puts the three money words — **sell,
beats, paid** — in Apple's #2-weighted field, and holds **live** so the keyword field
doesn't have to.

### Keywords (max 100, no spaces after commas)
**`artist,producer,beatmaker,type beat,trap,rapper,singer,song,indie,rap,stream,collab,community,dj`**  *(96)*

Changes from the shipped set, in the house style:
- **Added `beatmaker`, `type beat`, `trap`** — `type beat` is *the* producer search (e.g.
  "drake type beat"); nothing else in the niche converts like it. `beatmaker` and `trap`
  are high-volume, low-overlap producer terms.
- **Dropped `beats`, `sell`, `live`** — now carried by the Subtitle, which Apple indexes;
  repeating them here would spend characters on ground we already hold.
- **Dropped `band`, `podcast`** — real but lower-intent for the wedge; cut to make room for
  the producer terms above. (Podcasts still surface via the Social category + description.)
- **Kept** artist, producer, rapper, singer, song, indie, rap, stream, collab, community, dj
  — the breadth terms that combine widely and cover the artist/listener side.
