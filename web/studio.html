Pro mic / interface: plug it in and pick it under Microphone — with Raw input the signal goes to the room untouched at studio quality. Connect your DAW: click Share app audio, pick the FL Studio / BandLab window or your screen, and tick "share audio" in the browser dialog. (Using ASIO? Route your DAW to the system output or share the entire screen with system audio.) When the host fires a sync count-in, this page counts 4 beats then flashes REC — punch record in your DAW on the flash and every take lines up.
