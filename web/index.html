What Laybell does

Built for music

Upload songs, podcasts and audiobooks with real cover art. Tag the producers, writers and features who worked on a track — they get credited, with a link back to their profile.

Go live

Broadcast to your followers from your phone, or run a listening session from the studio. Fans can tip you live, and everyone in the room sees it.

Sell your work

List beats and songs on your own storefront. Sell them outright, lease them, or give them away to fans who follow you. Buyers get the files instantly.

Get paid

Tips and sales land in one wallet and pay out to your bank account. Your earnings are yours.

About

Laybell LLC is a Maryland limited liability company building Laybell, a music and social platform for independent artists. The app runs in ten languages and is available now on the App Store and Google Play.

Laybell operates a registered DMCA agent, is a registered electronic service provider with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, and holds a public performance licence with BMI.
